## SLIDE 2: Current Challenges in Sports Event Management

### Pain Points We Address:

**❌ Check-in/Check-out Tracking**
- No real-time visibility into when teams arrive at hotels
- Manual processes lead to confusion and delays

**❌ Extended Stay Management**
- Unable to track early checkouts or players staying beyond allocated time
- Leading to resource wastage

**❌ Transit Hotel Management**
- Lack of integrity and transparency in hotel accommodation processes
- Leading to disputes and inefficiencies

**❌ Limited Communication**
- Difficulty in sending targeted notifications to specific teams, coaches, or all participants simultaneously

**❌ Travel Coordination**
- Poor advance communication with traveling teams results in last-minute coordination issues

**❌ Manual Oversight**
- Paper-based processes require extensive manual supervision and are prone to errors
