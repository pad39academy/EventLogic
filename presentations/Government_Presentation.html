Current Challenges in Sports Event Management

❌ Check-in/Check-out Tracking

No real-time visibility into when teams arrive at hotels. Manual processes lead to confusion and delays.

❌ Extended Stay Management

Unable to track early checkouts or players staying beyond allocated time, leading to resource wastage.

❌ Limited Communication

Difficulty in sending targeted notifications to specific teams, coaches, or all participants simultaneously.

❌ Transit Hotel Management

Lack of integrity and transparency in hotel accommodation processes, leading to disputes and inefficiencies.

❌ Travel Coordination

Poor advance communication with traveling teams results in last-minute coordination issues.

❌ Manual Oversight

Paper-based processes require extensive manual supervision and are prone to errors.
